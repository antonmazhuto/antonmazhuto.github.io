Privacy policy: steam-monitoring

Last updated: November 8, 2026.

steam-monitoring is a private tool run by Anton Mazhuto. It collects public data from the Steam storefront and saves the results to the Google Drive of the person who authorised it.

What the app accesses

The app asks for one Google permission: drive.file. With it the app can create files in your Google Drive and read or change only the files it created itself. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

What the app does with your data

  • It uploads its own result files (tables and screenshots of public Steam pages) to a folder it creates in your Drive.
  • It does not read, collect or store your personal data, contacts, email or other files.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Access tokens

The authorisation token is stored as an encrypted secret of the private repository that runs the tool and is used only to upload result files. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
